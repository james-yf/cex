Leading and trailing dots are valid for decimals. E.g., .5 == 0.5 and 5. == 5.0.
 
The symbol for exponentiation is '^'. E.g., 2^3 and 2^-3 are valid expressions.  

Scientific notation is not supported. E.g., 2e+3 is considered erroneous and will not be treated as 2 * 10^3.

Only base 10 numbers are allowed. No binary, hexadecimal, octal, etc. E.g., 0b101, 0xFFFF and 0234/0o234 are considered
erroneous and will not be treated as valid numbers.
